Getting started

How do I send my first invoice?

Tap New invoice, add your client, add what you did and its price, then tap Preview & send. Choose Email, Share PDF or Print. The PDF is sent from your own Mail or Messages, so it arrives from you as a normal attachment.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Kindly has no account and works offline. You only need a connection when you actually send an email or message, or when you buy or restore Kindly Pro.

How do I add my logo, signature or payment details?

Open Settings → Business details for your logo and signature, and Settings → Payment details for bank details or a pay link. A pay link is also printed as a "Scan to pay" QR code on unpaid invoices.

How do I change how my invoices look?

Open Settings → Invoice style, or the ⋯ menu on any document and choose Change style. Pick one of five templates and an accent colour. The style applies to all your documents.

How do estimates work?

Create an estimate in the Estimates tab and send it. When your client accepts, open it and tap To invoice. Kindly copies everything into a new invoice.

How do I mark an invoice as paid, or record a part payment?

Open the invoice and tap Mark paid. Change the amount to record a part payment. A fully paid invoice becomes a receipt you can send. You can also swipe right on an invoice in the list.

Kindly Pro and billing

What is free and what does Kindly Pro add?

Everything in Kindly is free to try, and you can send two documents at no cost. Kindly Pro makes that unlimited. There are no tiers and no monthly caps.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. You are not charged during the trial. If you allow notifications, Kindly reminds you two days before it ends. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the end, the yearly plan begins and Apple charges the price shown when you started.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kindly and tap Cancel Subscription, or use Settings → Manage subscription inside Kindly. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. After cancelling you keep Kindly Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and your documents stay on your iPhone.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose "Request a refund" and pick Kindly. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, but email us if you get stuck and we will help.

I paid but the app still says "Free plan".

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you bought with. If that does not help, email us and include the receipt email from Apple.

What happens to my invoices if I stop paying?

Nothing is locked. Everything you created stays viewable, and documents you already sent can still be opened, shared and exported. Sending new documents beyond the free allowance needs Kindly Pro.

Your data

Where are my invoices stored?

On your iPhone only. Kindly has no servers. Your data is included in your normal iCloud or computer backup of the iPhone.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

If you restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, Kindly's data comes with it. You can also use Settings → Save a backup file on the old phone, send the file to the new one, and use Settings → Restore from a backup. Then tap Restore purchases.

How do I get my data out for my accountant?

Use Settings → Export spreadsheet (CSV). It lists every invoice and estimate with dates, totals, tax and payment status, and opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Erase all data on this iPhone, or delete the app. We hold none of your data, so there is nothing else to remove.

Troubleshooting

The Email option is missing when I tap Send.

The Email option appears when Apple's Mail app is set up on your iPhone. If you use another mail app, tap Share PDF and choose it from the share sheet.

I am not getting due-date reminders.

Check that Settings → Due-date reminders is on inside Kindly and that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Kindly. Reminders are set when you send an invoice that has a due date.

My totals or tax look wrong.

Open the document's Edit tab and check the tax rate, the discount, and whether each item is marked taxable. If it still looks wrong, email us a description of the items and rates and we will look into it.
