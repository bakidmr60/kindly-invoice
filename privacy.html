Privacy Policy

Kindly — Invoice Maker for iPhone · Effective 20 November 2026

The short version. Kindly does not collect, upload, sell or share your data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Your invoices, estimates, clients and business details are stored on your iPhone and nowhere else.

1. Who we are

Kindly ("the app") is published by an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at baki.glaurung@gmail.com.

2. Information the app handles

To do its job, Kindly stores the information you type into it:

  • your business details (name, address, contact details, tax or registration number, logo, signature, payment instructions and pay link);
  • your clients' details (name, email, phone, address, notes);
  • your documents (invoices, estimates, receipts, line items, amounts, dates and payment status) and your saved items.

All of this is stored locally on your device, inside the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or to any server operated by us. We cannot see it, read it or recover it.

3. Information we collect

None. Kindly contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software and no third-party SDKs. It does not create an identifier for you, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use your data for advertising. The App Store privacy label for Kindly is "Data Not Collected".

4. Device features the app uses

FeatureWhen and why
PhotosOnly when you choose a logo. Kindly uses Apple's system photo picker, so it receives only the single image you select and never has access to your library.
ContactsOnly when you tap "Import from Contacts". Kindly uses Apple's system contact picker and receives only the one contact you select.
NotificationsOnly if you allow them. Reminders about due dates and about a free trial ending are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server.
Mail, Messages, sharing and printingWhen you send a document, iOS hands the PDF to the app you choose (for example Mail, Messages or WhatsApp). That app's own privacy policy applies to what you send through it.
FilesWhen you export a spreadsheet or a backup file, or restore from a backup you pick.

5. Purchases

Kindly Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never receive your name, email address, card details or billing address. The app only learns from Apple whether a purchase is active on your device. Apple's handling of your payment information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Backups and where your data lives

  • Your data is included in the device backups that iOS makes to iCloud or to your computer, if you have those turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple, not by us.
  • If you use "Save a backup file" or "Export spreadsheet", the app creates a file and you decide where it goes. Keep those files somewhere safe: they contain your business and client information.
  • Payment links you add (for example a PayPal link) are printed on your invoices as text and as a QR code. Kindly does not contact those services.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete a single document or client inside the app, erase everything with Settings → Erase all data on this iPhone, or remove the app, which deletes its storage. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to answer you, we do not add you to any mailing list, and you can ask us to delete the correspondence at any time.

9. Crash and usage reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and performance reports. These reports do not identify you and do not contain your invoices or client information. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

10. Children

Kindly is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA give you rights over personal data that an organization holds about you. Because Kindly keeps your data on your own device and we do not collect it, you already hold and control it directly. We do not sell or share personal information. For the support emails described in section 8, you can ask us for access, correction or deletion by writing to the address above.

12. Your clients' information

You are responsible for the client information you store in the app and for complying with the laws that apply to your business when you do. Kindly helps by keeping that information on your device only.

13. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Kindly changes how data is handled, we will update this page and change the effective date before the change reaches you. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email baki.glaurung@gmail.com.
